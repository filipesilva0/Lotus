## 0n-voice. Voice studio with Lotus's own engine: everything Voicebox did (16 Nov, 0.8.2) (⚠️ cloning not run with real models)

  - **Voices**: make a voice by copying one from 5–15 s of speech (record with a reading script, or upload; quality check and trim from `SampleHealthPanel`), or tune a built-in speaker; each voice has a test line, In Use, recording swap, words in the recording (ZipVoice, written by Whisper), quality steps or speed, its own effects, notes.   - **Speech to Text** (was Dictate): drop or choose a recording of any length (audio or video), or record. `voice/stt.ts` decodes it at 16 kHz mono, cuts it at quiet moments into parts Whisper can hear (`splitPoints`, ≤ 28 s), writes them down one by one with live progress and time left, and keeps running on other pages (Stop keeps what was written). - **Effects** run in the window (`voice/render.ts`, OfflineAudioContext): volume, bass, treble, tape pitch, room, echo, robot, phone/radio band, fades, even loudness; 10 presets. Pure logic in `voice/studio.ts`.

### 0m-win. 0.7.0 on Windows: "background core isn't running" (16 Nov)
The installed 0.7.0 showed the notice with no reason, even after reopening.
