Lotus now suggests models for your kind of computer, from a laptop with no graphics card to a 128 GB Mac, with 27 chat models, smaller image models that fit 6–8 GB cards, more voices and every Whisper size. Every page now has the same layout: no bar across the top.

## New
- **Models for your computer**: Lotus sorts this computer into a class (no graphics card, 4–6, 8–10, 12–16, 24–32 or 48+ GB, or a Mac by its memory) and shows three chat and three image picks for it first, in the first-run guide and on the Models page. With Ollama, the guide suggests the same chat models.
- **27 chat models**: Qwen 3.5 (0.8B to 122B), Qwen 3.6 and 3.8 27B, Gemma 4 (E2B to 31B), NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Bolt and Lightning, OpenAI gpt-oss 20B and 120B, Phi-4 Mini, Mistral Small 3.2, Qwen 3 14B and Qwen 3 Coder. Only Qwen 3 8B and Nemotron 3 Nano 4B have passed Lotus's real tasks so far.
- **Smaller image models** for Lotus's own engine: FLUX.2 Klein 4B and 9B, Z-Image Turbo and Qwen-Image in compact files, each with its own text encoder and VAE. On an 8 GB card the suggestion is now Z-Image Turbo (small). FLUX.2 Klein 9B is for personal, non-commercial use only.
- **More voices**: Piper voices in 14 languages, including two more European Portuguese voices.
- **Every Whisper size** for dictation and speech to text: Tiny, Base, Small, Medium, Turbo and Large v3.
- **Very big models** that Hugging Face splits into parts download whole.

## Changed
- **The same layout everywhere**: Tasks, the Models page and Voice (with Voicebox) no longer have a bar across the top. Their sections are in the sidebar, like Chat and Images, and Voice no longer shows an engine status.
- **Voice with Voicebox** uses the same tab names as Lotus's own voice engine: Speak, Voices, Stories, Dictation, History.
- **Without a graphics card**, Lotus uses this computer's memory to say whether a model can load at all, and lists smaller (faster) models first.

## Fixes
- Gemma 4 is shown with its real licence (Apache 2.0).
- A Hugging Face model that only has other sizes is no longer downloaded by mistake when you ask for one by name.
