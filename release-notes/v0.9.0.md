Voice no longer needs Voicebox: Lotus's own voice engine can now speak, copy voices, make multi-voice stories and turn long recordings into text you can summarize in Chat.

## Voice
- **Speak**: any voice, long texts, effects (presets like Warm Narrator, Phone Call, Big Hall, Robot, plus every setting), read a text file aloud, favourites.
- **Voices**: copy a voice from 5–15 seconds of speech (record or upload, with a quality check and trim), or tune a built-in voice. The voice you pick also reads Lotus's spoken questions and notices. Get **PocketTTS** or **ZipVoice** in Models to copy voices.
- **Stories**: write a script like "Ana: Hello", give each speaker a voice, arrange the lines on four tracks, play them together and export one WAV.
- **Speech to Text** (was Dictate): drop a recording of any length, audio or video. Lotus cuts it at quiet moments, writes it down with live progress (it keeps going on other pages), and saves it with timestamps. Copy it, download .txt or .srt subtitles, or **Open in Chat** to summarize it, list key points and action items, or turn it into meeting notes.
- The page now looks like the other pages: sidebar, chat bar and a Settings panel.

## Work
- Attach files (button or drag and drop): images go to the model, text and code files are sent with your request.
- The sidebar matches Chat's, and the chat bar shows the video-memory bar.

## Images
- With Lotus's own image engine, the progress card shows real steps ("Step 12 of 30") and a real progress bar.
- The video-memory bar reads your graphics card directly, so it works without ComfyUI.

## Known limits
- Voice copying and long transcriptions have not been tried with the real models yet; tell us how they sound and how long they take.
- If a transcript is bigger than the chat model's context window, Chat sees only part of it (Lotus warns you).
