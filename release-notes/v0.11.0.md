Work now deploys agents by itself and shows each one with its task and progress, in the page and on the island. Chat and Work read PDFs, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and other common files. Skills, Memory, Soul and Plugins are tabs on the Tasks page, and the profile button opens your usage with Hardware & Runtime.

## New
- **Agents in Work**: when a job has parts that don't depend on each other, Lotus starts several agents at once, each with its own face, task and progress ("3 agents · 2 of 3 done"). Open an agent to see its steps and what it returned. They also appear on the island while they work.
- **Thinking in Work**: turn on Think in the Work bar and the model's reasoning shows above the reply, as in Chat.
- **Edit, Try Again, Copy and Read Aloud in Work**, on your requests and Lotus's replies.
- **More files you can attach** in Chat, Work and the island: PDF, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), PowerPoint (.pptx), OpenDocument (.odt, .ods, .odp), EPUB, RTF and HTML, besides images, text and code. Everything is read on this computer. Scanned PDFs without text say so (attach the pages as images instead).

## Changed
- **Tasks page tabs**: Board, Routines, Skills, Memory, Soul, Plugins. Skills shows yours (to edit) or all of them, including the built-in ones.
- **Profile button** opens your profile straight away: usage, plus **Hardware & Runtime** (moved there from Engines → Models). Sign in and out on that page.
- **Chat's Model panel** is simpler and roomier: the Advanced part (system prompt, raw request) is gone; stop sequences stay. The system prompt is edited in Tasks → Soul.

## Fixes
- **Image cards and question cards** now appear even when the model writes their JSON a little differently (a ```json block, no block at all, a trailing comma, the prompt above the settings). Before, you could get the prompt and the JSON but no card to press.
- **Number fields** (context window, steps, guidance, LoRA strength…) have arrows that fit; they looked cut off.
