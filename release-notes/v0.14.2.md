Big image models work again on 8 GB graphics cards, and a model's thinking no longer spills into its answer in Chat.

## Fixes
- **Images on 8 GB cards**: SDXL models (Juggernaut XL and others) stopped with "The image needed 1.2 GB of video memory… only 0.8 GB was free". Lotus's image engine now leaves 2 GB of the card free instead of 1 (Windows and the engine's own buffers need it), and if it still runs out, Lotus tries once more with 3 GB free and the prompt and negative prompt run one after the other.
- **Thinking in the answer**: with Lotus's own chat engine, some models (newer Qwen and Gemma) streamed their thinking into the answer, and Lotus's "did it really do that?" check then wiped the answer and wrote it again. The engine now separates thinking from the answer, and Lotus moves any thinking that still arrives in the answer (from Ollama or your own server too) to the thinking panel.
