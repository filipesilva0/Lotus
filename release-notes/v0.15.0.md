Voice calls feel more like talking to a person: you can cut Lotus off just by speaking, it answers sooner, and you can give it a much more natural voice with GPT-SoVITS.

## New
- **Interrupt by voice**: on a call, Lotus keeps listening while it thinks and talks. Start speaking and it stops at once; what you said becomes your next turn, including the first words. Lotus's voice goes through the same echo cancellation video calls use, so it doesn't hear itself on speakers. Headphones still work best. The Interrupt button works as before, and you can turn this off on the Call page ("Interrupt by voice").
- **GPT-SoVITS voices** (optional, free): for the most natural copied voice, download GPT-SoVITS (a separate program of several GB), then pick its folder in Voice → Voices → Make a Voice. "GPT-SoVITS" then appears as a cloning model, and Lotus starts it when a voice made with it speaks. It speaks English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Cantonese, and runs best on an NVIDIA graphics card.

## Faster calls
- Whisper stays loaded during a call instead of starting again for every sentence you say.
- The next sentence's audio is made while the current one plays, so there are no pauses between sentences.
- A long first sentence starts playing at its first comma.
- Lotus answers after a shorter pause (0.75 s instead of 0.9 s).
- When a call starts, Lotus loads Whisper and the voice while you say hello.
