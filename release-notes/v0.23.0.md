A new island: push your mouse to the top of the screen for quick buttons, notes, what's playing, what's next, and a shelf for files that Lotus can summarise, convert or send to a page.

## New
- **A redesigned island**: white and floating in light mode, black and growing out of the top of the screen in dark mode (Settings → Island → Colour). One accent colour, bigger buttons, clearer text, and a screen reader hears changes without the timer ticking.
- **Open it with the mouse**: push the mouse against the top centre of any screen and the island opens there, on the screen you are using. Turn it off in Settings → Island → Open with the mouse.
- **Quick buttons**: Ask, Dictate, Call, Summarise what you copied, Focus, and more. Edit picks up to six, including your own skills and routines.
- **Notes into memory**: type in the island's box, then keep it in memory or add it to your tasks. Enter asks Lotus instead.
- **Drop files on the island**: drag a file to the top of the screen and drop it on Keep for later, Ask Lotus, Turn into… or Use in…. Kept files wait on the shelf (Lotus Studio/Shelf). Lotus can turn videos into MP4, GIF or MP3, sound into MP3 or WAV, write down what is said, or send the file to Work, Images, Video or Voice. Converting needs FFmpeg (Settings → Models).
- **Now playing**: the song or video playing on your PC, with play, pause and skip (Windows). Your music pauses during calls, dictation and read-aloud, then plays again.
- **Copied text** (off until you turn it on): after you copy some text, the island offers Explain, Summarise, Translate, Fix wording and Read aloud. Copies from password managers and from Lotus itself are ignored.
- **Focus timer**: start one from the island, or with the timer button on a task. When time is up, choose Done (the task moves to Done) or 5 more minutes.
- **Next up**: tasks due today, the next routine, and, if you paste your calendar's private address in Settings → Island, your next meeting with a Join button.
