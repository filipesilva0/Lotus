Memory now works more like yours: long-term, short-term and notes for each chat. Lotus sends only the memories that fit your message, so replies start faster on smaller graphics cards.

## New: memory in three layers
- **Long-term:** lasting facts about you and your standing rules. Everything Lotus remembered before this update is here, unchanged.
- **Short-term:** what is going on lately (a trip, a deadline, the project you are on). It fades after 14 days unless it keeps coming up. If it comes up again and again, it moves to long-term.
- **Chat notes:** notes that belong to one chat. Only that chat sees them, and they go when you delete the chat.
- **Only what fits your message goes along.** Before each reply Lotus searches its memory and sends just the relevant parts (at most about 350 tokens), instead of everything every time. Your standing rules always go along.
- **The model can look things up.** When it needs more, it searches all three layers and your past chats by itself.
- **Daily tidy-up.** Once a day Lotus merges duplicates, lets old short-term notes fade and keeps what matters. It never removes anything you saved yourself. There is also a "Tidy now" button.
- **Assistant → Memory** shows the three layers, with search, add, move between layers and forget (with Undo).

## Good to know
- The new memory has not been tried on a real graphics card yet. If Lotus forgets or recalls something oddly, please tell us.
