Lotus makes videos, writes real Word, Excel and PowerPoint files, and comes with built-in skills: narrate your own video or film new footage on your graphics card, get finished Office files from Work, and let ready-made plugins help with design, documents, writing, code and logos.

## New: Video
- **A Video page** (Ctrl+5, after Voice). Choose "Narrate my video", "From my pictures" or "From an idea", say what it should say, pick a voice (or let Lotus choose) and the shape (wide, phone or square). The job runs in Work, where you can watch each step and ask for changes. Your finished videos are listed on the page and play right there.
- **Work mode can make videos.** It can watch a video (a sheet of frames for chat models that see pictures, plus what is said, written down by Whisper), write a script that fits, choose the best of your voices, record the voiceover with subtitles, and put pictures and clips together with slow pans, zooms and crossfades. Narrating a video lowers or mutes its own sound under the voice.
- **Your voices.** Videos use the same voices as Voice: the built-in ones and your own. A voice copied from a recording is only used when you ticked the consent promise for it, and follows Lotus Pro like everywhere else.
- **Videos play in Work.** The Artifacts panel plays videos and sounds and shows pictures.
- **A Video plugin** with skills for narrated videos, narrating your own video and describing a video, plus the /video and /narrate commands. It's Lotus's own: the onetake skill you may know is for non-commercial use only, so none of it is copied.
- **AI-filmed footage.** On the Video page, "Filmed by AI" makes real new moving footage on your own graphics card with the open Wan video models (Apache-2.0), and Work mode can do it too (video.generate, /film). Lotus picks the model for your card: Wan 2.1 1.3B for 6–11 GB, Wan 2.2 5B for 12–23 GB (which can also bring your pictures to life), and Wan 2.2 A14B for 24 GB cards. Get them in Settings → Models → Video.
- **Slow, but it doesn't crash.** On an 8 GB card each 2-second clip takes several minutes, and Lotus tells you how long before it starts. The model waits in your computer's memory while the card works, the video is decoded in pieces, and if the card runs short of memory Lotus tries again with smaller settings instead of failing. A run that stops answering is stopped, you can stop any run yourself, and when a computer can't film (no graphics card, or too little memory) Lotus says why and uses moving pictures instead.
- **FFmpeg** joins the Lotus Engines (Settings → Models, about 165 MB, the LGPL build). If FFmpeg is already on your computer, Lotus uses it.

## New: built-in skills and plugins
- **Built-in plugins.** Lotus now comes with ready-made plugins: Design, Documents, Writing, Code Quality, Toolkit, Superpowers and Site Audit (plus Caveman, off by default). Find them on the Assistant page under Plugins. A plugin's skills only join in when your message is about the same thing, so they don't get in the way.
- **Logo design.** The Design plugin can design a logo or app icon with you (/logo), and Work mode can turn it into PNG files at the sizes stores and websites need, plus a favicon.
- **Globe.** A new Globe plugin shows what is happening in the world right now (earthquakes, flights, satellites, storms, weather) from free public feeds, with /globe and /globe-map. It also works with the God's Eye View app if you have it. Lotus can now read JSON and CSV data feeds from the web.
- Plugin skills and commands now work in Work mode too, not only in Chat.

## Work mode
- **Real Office files.** Work can write real Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx, with formulas and several sheets) and PowerPoint (.pptx) files. Finished replies show each file as a card you can open in its usual app.
- Long code goes into files instead of filling the answer.
- The files panel on the right can be hidden, the message box is the same as Chat's, and Think sits in the model pill.

## A calmer look
- **One accent colour** across the whole app instead of a colour per page.
- **Tasks and Assistant.** Tasks is now just Board, Routines and Activity. Memory, Soul, Skills and Plugins moved to a new Assistant page.

## Bigger models on smaller cards
- Large "expert" models that don't fit your graphics card now run split between the card and your computer's memory, instead of not starting.
- Inkling 2 Mini is in the model list. It needs a newer llama.cpp engine than is out today: when one is released, click Update on the engine in Settings → Models and it will run.

## Good to know
- AI footage takes minutes per second of video on an 8 GB card (about an hour and a half for 30 seconds). Moving pictures are still the quick choice. Video models can't write text, so titles and words on screen still come from pictures and subtitles.
- To let Lotus see your video, use a chat model that reads images (for example Gemma 3 4B or Qwen 2.5 VL 7B, both fit 8 GB). Without one, it works from what is said.
- Videos are made with your NVIDIA card's video encoder when there is one, so a minute of video takes seconds.
