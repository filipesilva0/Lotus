Microphones that Lotus couldn't find now work, with a microphone picker and check in Voice. Chat can now schedule routines, make to-do lists, change its own soul when you ask, and offer to create the images it writes prompts for.

## Fixes
- **"No microphone found"**: Lotus now tries the microphone you picked, then the system's default, then every microphone it can see, before giving up. When it still can't, it says which setting to check (on Windows: Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone, including "Let desktop apps access your microphone").
- **Microphone on macOS**: the app now tells macOS why it wants the microphone, so it can be allowed in System Settings.

## New
- **Pick and check your microphone**: Voice → Settings (right panel) → Microphone. Check Microphone listens for a few seconds and tells you whether it heard you. The choice is used for calls, recordings, dictation and spoken answers.
- **Routines and to-do lists from Chat**: ask "every weekday at 8:30, summarize my day" or "make a to-do list for moving house" and Lotus adds it to Tasks: scheduled routines (cron jobs), to-do lists as one checklist or one task per item, and single tasks as before.
- **Lotus can change its soul**: ask it to be different from now on ("be more direct", "call yourself Ana", "always answer in European Portuguese") and it updates its soul, the system prompt every chat starts with. It asks before each change, and "undo that" brings the previous version back. Not in incognito or memory-off chats.
- **Create images from Chat**: when you ask Chat for an image or an image prompt, it now asks "Create it in Images now?" with Yes and No. It picks the model, shape, size, number of images, steps and guidance for that picture; Yes opens Images with those settings and starts it.

## Changed
- **Example prompts** show only their titles; click one to put the whole prompt in the box.
