The island is smoother: it shows what Lotus is really doing, opens and closes with an animation, folds away quickly, and can be frosted glass.

## Better
- **While Lotus works, the island is about that work**: what it is doing now, how far along, how long it has taken, and anything else running. Menu goes to the quick buttons, now playing and the note box; tap the top of the menu to go back.
- **It knows when Lotus is busy**: chats that are thinking, tasks, routines and Work runs show on the island, even when they go quiet for a while. Before, it could say "Nothing running right now" while Lotus was working.
- **Opening and closing animation**: the island grows out of the top of the screen and folds back into it.
- **Folds away faster** once the mouse leaves it.
- **Frosted glass**: a new colour in Settings → Island → Colour, see-through, light or dark with Lotus.
- **Smoother edges**: the shadow is no longer cut off into straight lines, and the black island's curved corners are smooth.
- The Lotus flower is on the island, as the Open Lotus button.
