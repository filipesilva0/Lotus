Rethink for Nemotron models, folders the AI can read and edit, Arena without Ollama, and fixes for Voice on Windows and the Models page.

## New
- **Rethink (Nemotron)**: in Chat's right panel (Model), choose Once, 2, 3 or 4 Times. After answering, the model checks its own answer for mistakes and gaps and writes a better version that many times; you see the last one, and the earlier versions stay under the reply.
- **Folders in Chat and Work**: Choose Folder now lets Lotus work in the folder: it opens, searches and edits the files it needs (saving asks first) instead of pasting every file into the conversation. A new editing step changes part of a file without rewriting the rest.
- **Arena & Benchmark works without Ollama**: it compares the chat models of the engine you use. With Lotus's own engine the models answer one after another, each loaded before its timer starts.

## Fixes
- **Voice on Windows**: "Failed to convert … to token IDs" with Piper voices. Lotus now gives the voice engine a folder path without spaces or accents, and if speaking still fails, the message shows the real reason.
- **Models page**: a download's progress bar no longer spills into the next card.
