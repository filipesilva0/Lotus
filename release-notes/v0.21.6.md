The Video page is now a video editor, calls play more smoothly and can use their own model, and calls no longer say every word twice.

## New: a video editor
- **The Video page (Ctrl+5) is a video editor:** your footage on the left, preview and timeline in the middle, tools on the right. The preview plays from light copies, so even long 4K files stay smooth.
- **One-click presets:** Captioned Short, Shorts from a long video, Clean up a talking video, Highlight reel, YouTube video and Square post. The AI makers (/narrate, /video, /film) are there too as "Made by AI" presets that run in Work.
- **Timeline:** drag clips to reorder, drag edges to trim, split (S), delete, set each clip's volume, add crossfades. Four shapes (16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5), with bars, a blurred copy behind, or crop for footage of another shape.
- **Captions** with real timings, in four looks (clean, bold, boxed, one word). You can edit them, burn them in or attach them.
- **Shorts:** Lotus finds the best moments of a long video, you tick which ones you want, and it makes tall 9:16 clips with captions.
- **Auto edit:** a highlight reel of your shots, or the pauses cut out of a talking video. The result lands on the timeline so you can tweak it, with Undo.
- **Voiceover and music:** write or generate a script, pick any Lotus voice, and the clips' own sound gets quieter under the voice. Add music that loops and fades out.
- **Export** uses your NVIDIA card's video encoder when there is one.
- The Video plugin gains /captions, /shorts and /autoedit.

## Calls
- **Smoother sound.** Calls keep a short buffer so speech no longer stutters while your PC is busy. With "Interrupt by voice" off, calls play straight out.
- **A model just for calls.** Voice → Call has its own model menu. Leave it on "Use Chat's model" to keep things as they were.

## Fixed
- Calls no longer say every word twice, and Chat's thinking no longer shows doubled words. Lotus sometimes connected to its engine twice at startup.

## Good to know
- Captions made by Whisper and exporting with the graphics card have not been tried on a real graphics card yet. If something looks off, please tell us.
