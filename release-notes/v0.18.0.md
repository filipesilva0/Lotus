Advanced mode is finished: use cloud models with your own key, connect MCP tool servers, and let other apps on your PC use Lotus through a local API. Lotus Pro is built in and switched off for now, so every feature stays free.

## New
- **Advanced mode**: Settings → General → Advanced mode shows the settings for people who know local AI. It's on if you used Lotus before, and everything in it is free.
- **Cloud models with your own key**: Settings → Advanced. Pick OpenAI, OpenRouter, Groq or Mistral, paste your key, and chat with their models. Your messages go to that service and it bills you; the key stays on your PC.
- **Tool servers (MCP)**: add a program (for example `npx -y @modelcontextprotocol/server-filesystem`) or a web address. Its tools work in Chat and Work, and Lotus asks before each use.
- **Local API**: turn it on and other apps on this PC can use Lotus's chat model at `http://127.0.0.1:11470/v1` (the OpenAI-style API), with a key you copy from Settings. Only this PC can reach it.

## Coming with Lotus Pro
- Lotus Pro (voice cloning, your own voices in calls and read-aloud, and multi-voice Stories) is ready inside the app but not on sale yet. Until it launches, these features stay free for everyone.
