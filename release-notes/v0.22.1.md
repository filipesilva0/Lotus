Video export with the blurred background (the tall Shorts look) works again. Think mode answers now appear once, after Lotus has checked them.

## Fixed
- **Video export with a blurred background.** Exporting a tall (Shorts-style) or square video with the blurred background failed with an FFmpeg error. The blur now uses a filter that the FFmpeg Lotus installs has. Lotus also now checks every video recipe against that FFmpeg, so this kind of mistake is caught before a release.
- **Think mode no longer rewrites its answer in front of you.** Lotus drafts and checks the answer inside the thinking drawer first, and only then puts it in the chat.
