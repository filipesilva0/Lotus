# Lotus privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

**Lotus collects nothing.** There is no account, no telemetry, no analytics and no Lotus server.
Your chats, settings, images, voices and tasks stay in the Lotus Studio folder on your own computer.

## What leaves your computer, and only when you choose it

- **Downloads you pick:** AI engines and models come straight from each project's own site
  (github.com, huggingface.co, ollama.com, download.comfy.org).
- **Web search and the browser tool:** only when you turn them on. Your search goes to the search
  service you pick, then to the pages it opens.
- **Cloud models:** only if you add your own API key. Your messages then go to that provider,
  under that provider's terms, paid by you.
- **Engines on another computer:** only if you point Lotus at one. Your requests go to that computer.
- **Sync:** only if you set up your own sync server and sign in. Nothing goes to a Lotus server.
- **Update check:** the GitHub download asks GitHub for new versions when you press the button
  (or at launch if you switch that on). The Microsoft Store copy is updated by the Store.

## Microphone

Lotus uses the microphone only for dictation and voice calls you start. Audio is turned into text
on your own computer and is not sent anywhere.

## Purchases

If you buy Lotus Pro in future, the payment and your purchase details are handled by the seller of
record (Polar), not stored by Lotus.

## Children

Lotus is not directed at children under 13.
