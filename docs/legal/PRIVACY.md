# Lotus privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

**Short version: Lotus collects nothing about you.** Everything you do in Lotus stays on your computer unless you choose to send it somewhere.

## What stays on your computer

Your chats, prompts, files, images, voices, recordings, memory, tasks and settings are stored in your `Lotus Studio` folder. The AI models run on your own computer. We have no servers that receive any of it, and Lotus has no analytics or tracking.

## When Lotus goes online

Lotus only connects to the internet for things you start or turn on:

- **Downloads and updates:** engines and models come from their makers' own pages (GitHub, Hugging Face, Ollama). Update checks read Lotus's public release page on GitHub. Those sites see your IP address, as with any download.
- **Web search and Deep Research:** when you turn them on, your search words go to the search engines and research sites listed in Settings and in THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md.
- **Cloud models:** if you add your own key for an online AI provider, what you send to that model goes to that provider under its own privacy policy.
- **Sign-in and sync:** optional. If you set up your own sync server, your profile is sent only to that server.

## Crash reports

Crash reports are **off** unless you turn them on in Settings › Privacy. When they are on and something goes wrong, Lotus shows you the exact report first. If you click Send, Lotus opens a new issue on Lotus's public GitHub page in your browser, and nothing is sent until you submit it there yourself. Reports hold the Lotus version, your operating system, graphics card and memory, which engines are installed, and the error. They never hold your chats, prompts, files or file names, and your user name is removed from folder paths. Reports on GitHub are public.

"Copy diagnostics" copies the same summary to your clipboard so you can paste it wherever you ask for help.

## Purchases

When Lotus Pro goes on sale, Polar (polar.sh) will sell it as the merchant of record. Polar handles your payment and email address under its own privacy policy. Lotus only stores the licence key on your computer and checks it with Polar.

## Children

Lotus is not made for children under 13, and you must be 18 or older to buy Lotus Pro.
